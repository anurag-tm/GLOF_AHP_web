Automated GLOF Susceptibility Model

This report details a Python-based geospatial workflow for estimating Glacial Lake Outburst Flood (GLOF) susceptibility. The model utilizes a Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) approach based on the Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP). By integrating high-resolution topographic data with temporal lake dynamics, it automatically calculates a Potential Risk Score Index (PRSI) and classifies hazard zones.

Scientific Basis & AHP Scoring

📊 Potential Risk Score Index (PRSI)

The final susceptibility of each glacial lake is computed via a weighted linear combination of five geomorphological and temporal parameters. Each parameter (\(V\)) is assigned a standardized risk score (0.25, 0.50, or 1.00) based on critical thresholds.

AHP Weighted Sum Equation
\[PRSI = (V_{Ava} \times 0.416) + (V_{Wat} \times 0.262) + (V_{Dist} \times 0.161) + (V_{Chg} \times 0.099) + (V_{Area} \times 0.062)\]

Parameter Breakdown:

  • \(V_{Ava}\) = Avalanche Area / Steep Slopes > 30° (Weight: 41.6%)
  • \(V_{Wat}\) = Local Watershed / Reach Area (Weight: 26.2%)
  • \(V_{Dist}\) = Distance to Active Glacier (Weight: 16.1%)
  • \(V_{Chg}\) = Lake Area Expansion Rate (Weight: 9.9%)
  • \(V_{Area}\) = Total Lake Area (Weight: 6.2%)

🔬 Topographic Extraction

The script uses a Sobel filter applied to a high-resolution DEM to derive surface slope. 2000-meter buffer zones around each lake serve as proxies for the local watershed. Pixels exceeding 30 degrees within this buffer are classified as potential mass-movement (avalanche) zones, representing the highest weighted trigger mechanism for GLOFs.

Geoprocessing Workflow

1

Vector Alignment & Filtering

Loads shapefiles (historical lakes, current lakes, glaciers), projects them to the DEM's CRS, and isolates active glaciers by filtering out features smaller than 0.1 km².

2

Spatial Join & Dynamics

Performs spatial joins between temporal lake layers to compute the expansion percentage. Measures the nearest distance from each lake to an active glacier.

3

Topographic Extraction

Masks the DEM using 2000m buffers around each lake. Calculates terrain slope via Sobel arrays to quantify total watershed area and avalanche-prone steep slopes (>30°).

✓

AHP Matrix & Visualization

Assigns threshold scores to all parameters, calculates the final PRSI, bins lakes into 5 Hazard Classes (Very Low to Very High), and generates maps, charts, and final shapefiles.
